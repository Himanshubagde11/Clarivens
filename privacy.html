Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Clarivens ("Clarivens", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us through our inquiry form, and is drafted with reference to the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you use our contact form or email us directly, including:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your company name, if provided
  • Any details you share about your project or purpose of contact

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, health records, or government identity numbers through this website. Please do not include such information in your messages to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss potential projects
  • Understand your requirements so we can propose suitable services
  • Maintain records of client communication for our own reference

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies and local browser storage to remember your cookie consent preference and, where applicable, to understand basic, aggregated website usage. These do not track you across other websites, and we do not use cookies for third-party advertising. You can decline non-essential cookies using the cookie banner shown on your first visit, and you can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

4. How Your Data is Stored and Secured

When you submit our contact form, the information you provide is sent securely to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, which forwards it to our email inbox and retains a copy so we can track and respond to enquiries. If our automated reply is enabled, EmailJS, another third-party service, is used solely to send you a confirmation email using the details you submitted. Neither service is authorized to use your data for any purpose beyond delivering it to us and, where applicable, sending that confirmation. As a backup, if either service is unavailable, your device may instead open a pre-filled email in your own email application to send directly to us.

Any correspondence you send us is stored in our business email account, protected using standard account security practices. While we and our service providers take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us, no method of transmission over the internet or email is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain correspondence and project-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage our business relationship with you, respond to your inquiry, and comply with applicable legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time as described in Section 7.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We use Formspree and EmailJS solely to process and respond to contact form submissions, as described in Section 4. Beyond these two service providers, we do not share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law, to comply with a valid legal process, or to protect our legal rights. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics services that track individual visitors on this website.

7. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable Indian law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request erasure of your personal data, subject to our legitimate business and legal obligations
  • Withdraw consent for us to process your data at any time
  • Raise a grievance regarding how your data has been handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer using the details in Section 9.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India, and any disputes arising from it are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of India.

This Privacy Policy is provided as a general reference for how Clarivens handles information and does not constitute legal advice. It has been prepared with reference to Indian data protection law but has not been reviewed by a licensed advocate. If you require a policy tailored to specific regulatory or contractual obligations, we recommend consulting a qualified legal professional.
