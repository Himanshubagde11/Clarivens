## Core Services

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- **What it is:** We take messy, unstructured, or inconsistent data and turn it into a clean, analysis-ready dataset.
- **Who needs it:** Any business whose data has duplicates, missing values, inconsistent formatting, wrong data types, or that was exported from multiple disconnected systems.
- **What we deliver:** A cleaned dataset file + a Data Quality Report showing what was fixed and why.
- **Typical use cases:** Cleaning CRM exports, fixing e-commerce sales data, merging records from multiple sources, standardizing date/currency formats.

### 2. Data Quality Assessment
- **What it is:** A comprehensive audit of a dataset's completeness, accuracy, consistency, and reliability.
- **Who needs it:** Businesses that need to trust their data before making decisions, or those who are worried their data might be flawed.
- **What we deliver:** A Data Quality Score (0–100) + a report with specific findings and remediation recommendations.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- **What it is:** A deep statistical exploration of a dataset to uncover patterns, correlations, outliers, and distribution characteristics.
- **Who needs it:** Businesses who want to understand what story their data tells before making decisions.
- **What we deliver:** An interactive EDA report with visualizations, statistical summaries, and key findings.

### 4. Sales Analytics
- **What it is:** Analysis of sales performance data to identify revenue drivers, declining trends, best/worst products, regional gaps, and growth opportunities.
- **Who needs it:** Sales managers, e-commerce businesses, retail operations, or any business tracking orders and revenue.
- **Typical questions answered:** "Why are our sales declining in Q3?", "Which products drive 80% of revenue?", "Which regions are underperforming?"

### 5. Customer Analytics
- **What it is:** Analysis of customer behavior, segmentation, retention, churn, and lifetime value.
- **Who needs it:** Businesses that want to understand who their best customers are, who is at risk of leaving, and how to improve retention.
- **Typical questions answered:** "Which customers are about to churn?", "Who are our top 20% customers?", "What is our customer lifetime value?"

### 6. Financial Analytics
- **What it is:** Analysis of profitability, margins, cost structures, and financial performance.
- **Who needs it:** Finance teams, CFOs, business owners tracking P&L.

### 7. Operational Analytics
- **What it is:** Analysis of internal processes — supply chain, logistics, inventory, staffing.
- **Who needs it:** Operations managers, logistics companies, manufacturers.

### 8. Marketing Analytics
- **What it is:** Analysis of campaign performance, channel effectiveness, attribution, and ROI.
- **Who needs it:** Marketing teams, digital agencies, e-commerce businesses.

### 9. Predictive Analytics & Forecasting
- **What it is:** Using historical data and ML models to forecast future trends — sales, demand, churn, revenue.
- **Who needs it:** Businesses that want to plan ahead with data-driven forecasts rather than guesswork.
- **Typical questions answered:** "What will our sales be next quarter?", "Which customers will churn next month?", "How much inventory should we stock?"

### 10. AI/ML Solutions
- **What it is:** Custom machine learning models, NLP solutions, recommendation engines, and anomaly detection systems.
- **Who needs it:** Businesses with large volumes of data that want automated intelligence built into their operations.

### 11. Business Intelligence & Dashboard Development
- **What it is:** Building interactive dashboards and reporting systems that give business users real-time visibility into KPIs.
- **Platforms:** Power BI, Tableau, custom web dashboards.
- **Who needs it:** Any business that currently relies on static Excel reports and wants live, interactive reporting.

### 12. Data Strategy Consulting
- **What it is:** Advisory services to help businesses build a data strategy — governance, infrastructure, team upskilling.
- **Who needs it:** Companies that want to become data-driven but don't know where to start.

## How the Clarivens Process Works

1. **Consultation:** Client describes their business problem to Clarivens AI or a human consultant.
2. **Data Upload:** Client uploads their dataset (CSV, Excel, JSON, etc.)
3. **Profiling:** Clarivens profiles the dataset — understanding its shape, quality, and analytics opportunities.
4. **Service Recommendation:** Based on the data profile + business problem, Clarivens recommends the right service package.
5. **Proposal:** A formal proposal with pricing is sent.
6. **Delivery:** Analysis is performed and results are delivered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does a project take?**
A: Depends on scope. Data cleaning for a medium dataset typically takes 2–5 business days. Full EDA + Insights takes 5–10 days. Custom ML projects take 3–8 weeks.

**Q: What file formats do you accept?**
A: CSV, Excel (.xlsx, .xls), JSON, Parquet, XML, and plain text. Upload limits are up to 50MB per file.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. All uploads are encrypted. We never share your data with third parties. Uploaded datasets are only used for the analysis you commissioned.

**Q: Do I need to know anything about data science?**
A: Not at all. Clarivens translates technical findings into clear business language that any manager or executive can act on.

**Q: Can Clarivens connect to our database or BI tool?**
A: Yes — for BI Dashboard Development projects we can connect directly to your database, data warehouse, or existing BI tool.

**Q: How do I get a quote?**
A: Share your business challenge and dataset details with Clarivens AI and a consultant will follow up with a detailed proposal and pricing within 24 hours.
