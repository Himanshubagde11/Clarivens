BI

Dashboards & BI

Interactive Power BI and Tableau dashboards built around the KPIs your team actually checks, so decisions don't wait on someone digging through spreadsheets. Every dashboard starts with understanding what question you're actually trying to answer, not just what data you happen to have.

Typical timeline1 to 3 weeks
Tools usedPower BI, Tableau, DAX, SQL

What's included

  • KPI discovery: a short working session to figure out what actually matters to track for your business
  • Data modeling so your dashboard stays fast and accurate as data grows, not a mess of one-off queries
  • Dashboard build with DAX measures, drill-through reports, slicers and interactive filters
  • Mobile-friendly layout so the numbers are readable on a phone during a meeting, not just a monitor
  • A walkthrough session so your team knows how to use, filter, and trust what they're looking at
  • One round of revisions after you've actually used it for a week and know what's missing

Ideal for: teams tracking sales, operations, or marketing metrics who are still relying on manual spreadsheet reports or scattered exports.

ETL

ETL & Data Pipelines

Azure Data Factory pipelines that pull data from your CSVs, APIs, and databases into one clean, reliable source of truth, with validation and error handling built in from day one, not bolted on after something breaks in production.

Typical timeline2 to 5 weeks
Tools usedAzure Data Factory, Azure SQL, Python, SQL

What's included

  • Mapping your current data sources and how they should flow together end to end
  • Parameterized ADF pipelines with scheduling and automated triggers, no manual exports
  • Data validation for schema, nulls and duplicates before data lands downstream
  • Error handling and alerting, so a failed run gets caught the same day, not the same month
  • A destination data model that's actually usable for reporting, not just a raw data dump
  • Documentation and data dictionaries so the pipeline isn't a black box only I understand

Ideal for: businesses pulling data from multiple systems by hand, or anyone tired of a monthly data-wrangling scramble before every report.

EDA

Data Cleaning & Analysis

Python-based cleaning, outlier detection and exploratory analysis, so decisions are based on data you can actually trust, not a spreadsheet full of guesses, typos, and duplicate rows nobody noticed.

Typical timeline3 days to 2 weeks
Tools usedPython, Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, SQL

What's included

  • Cleaning and standardizing messy, inconsistent, or duplicated records across your dataset
  • Outlier and anomaly detection so unusual numbers get flagged instead of skewing your averages
  • Exploratory analysis to surface trends, patterns and data quality issues you didn't know were there
  • A clear written summary of findings in plain language, not just a pile of unlabeled charts
  • Recommendations on what to track going forward to keep the data clean without redoing this later
  • Cleaned dataset handed back to you in a usable format, ready for reporting or modeling

Ideal for: anyone sitting on a dataset they don't fully trust yet, or who needs a one-time deep dive before making a decision.

Reporting & Automation

Automated recurring reports and SQL stored procedures, so your team stops manually rebuilding the same spreadsheet every week or month, and stops finding out about a broken number after it's already gone out.

Typical timeline1 to 3 weeks
Tools usedAzure Data Factory, SQL Server, Power BI, Excel

What's included

  • Auditing your current recurring report and mapping exactly where the manual effort goes
  • Automated workflows using ADF triggers and SQL stored procedures instead of manual steps
  • Scheduled delivery so the report is ready and in the right inbox before anyone has to ask
  • A fallback plan and error logging so failures get caught and flagged, not silently missed
  • Version history so you can see what changed between reports without digging through email
  • A short handover session so your team can adjust minor things without needing to call me first

Ideal for: teams that already know what report they need, they just don't want to build it by hand every single cycle.
