Every project starts with a short conversation about your data and your goal. Below is a detailed look at what each service actually includes.

BI

Dashboards & BI

Interactive Power BI and Tableau dashboards built around the KPIs your team actually checks, so decisions don't wait on someone digging through spreadsheets.

What's included

  • KPI discovery: figuring out what actually matters to track for your business
  • Dashboard build with DAX measures, drill-through reports, slicers and filters
  • Clean visual design so the numbers are readable at a glance, not just accurate
  • A walkthrough session so your team knows how to use and read it

Ideal for: teams tracking sales, operations, or marketing metrics who are still relying on manual spreadsheet reports or scattered exports.

ETL

ETL & Data Pipelines

Azure Data Factory pipelines that pull data from your CSVs, APIs, and databases into one clean, reliable source of truth, with validation and error handling built in from day one.

What's included

  • Mapping your current data sources and how they should flow together
  • Parameterized ADF pipelines with scheduling and automated triggers
  • Data validation for schema, nulls and duplicates before data lands downstream
  • Documentation and data dictionaries so the pipeline isn't a black box

Ideal for: businesses pulling data from multiple systems by hand, or anyone tired of a monthly data-wrangling scramble.

EDA

Data Cleaning & Analysis

Python-based cleaning, outlier detection and exploratory analysis, so decisions are based on data you can actually trust, not a spreadsheet full of guesses and typos.

What's included

  • Cleaning and standardizing messy, inconsistent, or duplicated records
  • Exploratory analysis to surface trends, outliers and data quality issues
  • A clear written summary of findings, not just a pile of charts
  • Recommendations on what to track going forward to keep data clean

Ideal for: anyone sitting on a dataset they don't fully trust yet, or who needs a one-time deep dive before making a decision.

AT

Reporting & Automation

Automated recurring reports and SQL stored procedures, so your team stops manually rebuilding the same spreadsheet every week or month.

What's included

  • Auditing your current recurring report and where the manual effort goes
  • Automated workflows using ADF triggers and SQL stored procedures
  • Scheduled delivery so the report is ready before anyone has to ask
  • A fallback plan and error logging so failures get caught, not missed

Ideal for: teams that already know what report they need, they just don't want to build it by hand every cycle.
