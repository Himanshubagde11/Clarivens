How We Work

A clear process, from first call to finished product.

No black boxes. Here's roughly how a project moves, tap a step to see what actually happens.

01

We talk through what data you have, what decisions it needs to support, and what "done" looks like. No jargon, no assumptions, just figuring out what you actually need.

02

For data work, I look at your actual sources, spreadsheets, databases, APIs, whatever it is. For a website or app, I map out the pages, features and platforms. Either way you get a simple plan: what needs doing, and roughly how long it'll take.

03

Pipelines get built, dashboards take shape, sites and apps come together, and you see working versions early, not just a final reveal. We adjust based on what actually looks and works well once it's in front of you.

04

You get full documentation, source files, and a walkthrough, so nothing lives only in my head. Light support is available afterward if something needs tuning as your data changes.
