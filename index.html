How We Work

A clear process, from first call to finished dashboard.

No black boxes. Here's roughly how a project moves, tap a step to see what actually happens.

01

We talk through what data you have, what decisions it needs to support, and what "done" looks like. No jargon, no assumptions, just figuring out what you actually need.

02

I look at your actual sources, spreadsheets, databases, APIs, whatever it is, and put together a simple plan: what needs cleaning, what needs connecting, and roughly how long it'll take.

03

Pipelines get built, dashboards take shape, and you see working versions early, not just a final reveal. We adjust based on what actually looks useful once it's in front of you.

04

You get full documentation, source files, and a walkthrough, so nothing lives only in my head. Light support is available afterward if something needs tuning as your data changes.

Built an automated Azure pipeline ingesting inventory data from flat files and REST APIs into Azure SQL Database, with parameterized ADF pipelines, error handling, and Python-based validation for schema, null, and duplicate checks, surfaced via near real-time Power BI dashboards.
