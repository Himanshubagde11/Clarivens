About

From mechanical engineering to making messy data make sense.

I'm a data analyst and the founder of Clarivens, with over three years of hands-on experience turning raw, disconnected data into ETL pipelines, optimized SQL and interactive dashboards that drive real business decisions, not just report on them.

I design Azure Data Factory pipelines that ingest and transform data from CSV, Excel, APIs and SQL sources into Azure SQL Database, work that has cut data processing time by 40% on active projects. I write T-SQL using joins, CTEs, window functions and stored procedures, build Power BI and Tableau dashboards with DAX measures and drill-through filters, and run exploratory data analysis in Python (Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn) on datasets north of 500K rows. Automating a single monthly reporting workflow alone cut manual effort by 60%.

Beyond dashboards, I've built customer segmentation models using RFM analysis and K-Means clustering, designed star-schema data warehouses, and automated recurring reports so teams stop rebuilding the same spreadsheet every month. Whether the data lives in Excel, a SQL Server, or scattered across APIs, I bring it into one place your team can actually trust.

More recently, Clarivens has grown to build the websites and apps that put that data to work too, so a client doesn't need a separate developer just to get their dashboard in front of the people who need it. Web and app projects use whatever technology actually fits the job, not a fixed stack applied to everything.

Earlier in my career I worked on CRM data customization and automation, a foundation in data modeling and business process design that still shapes how I approach every project today. My background in mechanical engineering taught me to trust the numbers over the story, and that discipline carries into every dashboard, pipeline, website and app I build.

The Manifesto

Why Clarivens exists.

Every business generates data. But data alone doesn't create value. The real value comes from understanding what the data is telling you: discovering patterns, identifying opportunities, and turning information into better decisions.

At Clarivens, we transform complex data into clear, meaningful insights that businesses can understand and act upon. We bring together analytics, technology, and problem-solving to help organizations make smarter decisions, improve performance, and uncover new opportunities.

Clarity means turning complex information into something meaningful. Innovation means finding better and smarter ways to understand and use data. Progress means turning insights into decisions, actions, and measurable results.

We don't believe analytics should simply produce reports and dashboards. Analytics should create direction. That's why we focus on understanding the problem first, finding the right insights, and delivering solutions that create real business value.

Today, our focus spans data analytics, web development and app development. Tomorrow, the possibilities are open, because technology will continue to evolve, businesses will continue to change, and new opportunities will continue to emerge.

Our purpose remains the same: create clarity, drive innovation, enable progress.
