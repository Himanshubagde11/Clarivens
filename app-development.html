Cross-Platform Apps

One app codebase that runs on both Android and iOS, a practical option when you need to reach both audiences without building and maintaining two separate apps.

Typical timeline4 to 8 weeks
Tools usedFlutter or React Native, chosen based on your app's requirements

What's included

  • A single codebase covering both major platforms
  • Shared business logic with platform-specific UI where it matters
  • Testing across both Android and iOS devices
  • Submission support for both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store
  • Lower long-term maintenance cost than building two native apps

Ideal for: startups and businesses that need to launch on both platforms without doubling the development budget.
